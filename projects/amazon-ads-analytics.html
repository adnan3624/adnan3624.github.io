Amazon Advertising Analytics

Campaign, keyword, search term and ASIN-level analysis of Amazon Ads with ACoS, ROAS and negative keyword discovery.

All data on this page is synthetic and illustrative. It demonstrates the method; it is not client data and the figures are not results.

Business context

A marketplace seller spends on Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands and needs to know which campaigns, search terms and ASINs earn their spend.

Problem statement

Amazon reports sales attributed to ads, but there is no customer-level journey as in a DTC store, and ad-attributed sales overlap with organic sales.

Data sources

  • Advertising API or console exports: campaign, targeting and search term reports
  • Business Reports by ASIN (sessions, units, total sales)
  • Product cost and fee data for margin

Data architecture

Reports are loaded daily into BigQuery, keyed by campaign, ad group, targeting, search term and ASIN. Reconciliation is done at campaign, ASIN and channel level, never by customer.

Technical implementation

SQL classifies search terms by spend and orders, flags terms with clicks above a threshold and no orders, and joins ad sales to total ASIN sales to estimate ad dependence (TACoS).

SQL methodology

-- negative keyword candidates (illustrative)
SELECT campaign, search_term,
  SUM(clicks) AS clicks, SUM(spend) AS spend, SUM(orders) AS orders
FROM stg_search_terms
WHERE report_date >= DATE_SUB(CURRENT_DATE(), INTERVAL 60 DAY)
GROUP BY 1,2
HAVING SUM(clicks) >= 20 AND SUM(orders) = 0
ORDER BY spend DESC;

Analytical outputs

  • Search term waste table
  • ACoS and ROAS by campaign and match type
  • ASIN view: ad sales, total sales, TACoS, contribution margin

Key findings

  • In the synthetic data, a small share of search terms holds most zero-order spend, making negative keyword cleanup the fastest lever.
  • High ACoS on an ASIN can be acceptable if margin and organic lift support it, so ACoS is read alongside TACoS and margin.

Business recommendations

  • Add exact negatives for repeat zero-order terms and review weekly.
  • Set ACoS targets per ASIN from margin, not one account-wide number.

Limitations and assumptions

Marketplace reporting does not expose customer-level data, and attribution windows differ from DTC platforms. Amazon figures should not be compared to GA4 or Meta numbers as if they were measured the same way.
